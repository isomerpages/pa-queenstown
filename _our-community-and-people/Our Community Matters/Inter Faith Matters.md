<h4><strong>Queenstown – A Community of Harmony and Respect</strong></h4>
<p>Queenstown is known not only for its rich heritage but also for its strong
commitment to <strong>interfaith understanding and harmony</strong>. As
one of Singapore’s most diverse estates, Queenstown actively promotes mutual
respect among residents of different races and religions through regular <strong>interfaith dialogues, community events, and inclusive initiatives</strong>.</p>
<p>These efforts aim to build bridges across communities, foster open conversations,
and strengthen the spirit of togetherness. In Queenstown, interfaith engagement
is more than a value—it’s a way of life that reflects the unity and resilience
of its people.</p>
