<h2><strong>A Community of Harmony and Respect</strong></h2>
<p></p>
<p>Queenstown is known not only for its rich heritage but also for its strong
commitment to interfaith understanding and harmony. As one of Singapore’s
most diverse estates, Queenstown actively promotes mutual respect among
residents of different races and religions through regular interfaith dialogues,
community events, and inclusive initiatives.</p>

<p>These efforts aim to build bridges across communities, foster open conversations,
and strengthen the spirit of togetherness. In Queenstown, interfaith engagement
is more than a value, it’s a way of life that reflects the unity and resilience
of its people.</p>
<h3>Harmony Dialogue 2023: Faithful Melodies</h3>
<p>Held in 2023, Faithful Melodies invited religious leaders and musicians
to share the role of music within their faith traditions,</p>
<p>While some traditions embrace music as an integral part of worship, others
take a more reserved stance, focusing instead on spoken word and silence
as spiritual expressions. What emerged was not only a deeper appreciation
of the different positions on music but a shared recognition of its power
to inspire, unite, and move the human spirit.</p>
<p>This session reminded us that even in our differences, we can listen and
in listening, we understand.</p>
<h3>Harmony Dialogue 2024: Faiths and Foods</h3>
<p>In 2024, we turned our attention to another important part of every culture:
food. With generous support from The Fullerton Hotel, <em>Faiths and Foods</em> gathered
representatives from various religions to explore how food plays a role
in spiritual life, from dietary laws and fasting practices to celebratory
meals.</p>
<p>Through honest conversation and shared meals, guests discovered not only
the <em>what</em> of different food practices but also the <em>why</em> behind
them. Food became more than nourishment; it became a window into belief,
community, and meaning.</p>
