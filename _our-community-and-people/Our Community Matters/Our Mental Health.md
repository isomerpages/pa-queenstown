<h2>Our Mental Health Matters</h2>
<p>COVID-19 changed more than just daily routines. Beyond the physical health
measures, masks, distancing, and restrictions. The pandemic highlighted
how critical mental wellbeing is to a resilient community. Seniors experienced
greater isolation, youth faced disruptions to school and social life, and
families had to adapt to new and often stressful home environments. Mental
health emerged as a key area needing sustained attention, not just during
the crisis, but as part of strengthening community support for the long
term.</p>
<h3>SafePod: Community-Based Mental Health Support</h3>
<p>Launched in 2021, SafePod is Singapore’s first mobile mental wellness
unit, aimed at raising awareness, reducing stigma, and improving access
to support in the community. In its first three years, SafePod partnered
Lion Befrienders to deploy Face AI technology, made 122 trips across Queenstown,
assessed nearly 5,000 individuals, and referred over 200 for further support.</p>
<p>In 2023, SafePod was refreshed with 19 partners to expand its reach. These
included mental health first aid training with Intellect, breath and meditation
workshops with Art of Living, creative therapy with Stabilo Singapore and
BeHuetiful People, and awareness programmes using films from the Mental
Health Film Festival.</p>

<h3>Colour Therapy Workshop </h3>
<p>The Colour Therapy Workshop is designed to help children and their parents
reconnect through the simple yet powerful act of creating art together.
Using colours as a medium for self-expression, the workshop encourages
meaningful conversations and emotional bonding.Beyond being an enjoyable
activity, colour therapy has been shown to support mental wellbeing. It
helps reduce stress, stimulates cognitive function, and provides a gentle
way to process emotions.</p>

<h3>Breathing Therapy Workshop</h3>
<p>The Breathing Therapy Workshop introduces participants to the Sky Breath®
Programme - an evidence-based breathing technique practised worldwide.
This guided session helps individuals take a break from daily stressors
and reconnect with themselves through simple, rhythmic breathing exercises.</p>

<h3>Conclusion </h3>
<p>Mental health is just as important as physical health - and seeking support
is a sign of strength, not weakness. Whether you're facing challenges or
simply want to better care for your wellbeing, help is available.</p>
