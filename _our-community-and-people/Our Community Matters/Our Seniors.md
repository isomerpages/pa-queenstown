<h2>Caring for Our Seniors</h2>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Seniors/WhatsApp_Image_2025_05_21_at_10_56_24.jpg">
</div>
<p>Singapore became a Blue Zone 2.0 in August 2023, reflecting how far we’ve
come in promoting healthy longevity. Life expectancy here is now 84.3 years.
But the reality is that many Singaporeans still spend their last 10 years
in poor health.</p>
<p>In Queenstown, 3 in 10 residents are aged 60 and above. That’s why supporting
our seniors is a key priority for my team and me.</p>
<hr>
<h3>Strengthening Our Ecosystem of Care</h3>
<p>In the past few years, we’ve partnered the Health District team to strengthen
support for seniors on multiple fronts.</p>
<p>We set up the Health District Satellite Office at 40 Margaret Drive to
bring services closer to residents.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Opening of Health District Satellite Office" src="/images/Seniors/photo_2025_05_21_10_43_54.jpg">
</div>
<p>In March 2025, we launched the Happy Village at Stirling, where a dedicated
Blended Care team supports the wellbeing of our seniors - physically, emotionally,
and socially.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Opening of Happy Village @ Mei Ling" src="/images/Seniors/NUHS150325_81.jpg">

<h3>Celebrating Seniors Through the Queenstown Seniors Festival</h3>
<p>One of the highlights last year was our inaugural Queenstown Seniors Festival
in August 2024. It was co-created with 13 public, private and people-sector
partners, and brought together more than 1,500 seniors and caregivers.</p>
<p>The festival showcased services, tools and opportunities that seniors
can tap on to lead empowered and fulfilling lives. It was heartening to
see so many residents explore what's possible in their senior years and
this is just the beginning. We will continue to organise more of such engagements
for our seniors in the years ahead.</p>

<h3>Making Queenstown More Dementia-Friendly</h3>
<p>Dementia affects 1 in 11 seniors in Singapore. In Queenstown, we’ve introduced
wayfinding murals at Stirling View and Margaret Drive to help seniors navigate
their neighbourhood more confidently.</p>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="Dementia Wayfinding Mural @ Margaret Drive" src="/images/Seniors/WhatsApp_Image_2025_05_21_at_10_50_59.jpg">
</div>
<p>In the coming years, we’ll expand these murals to more precincts. We’re
also working with agencies to establish more dementia Go-To Points that
are safe, familiar community spots like Blk 40 kopitiam - so that families
can feel assured knowing help is close by.</p>
<hr>
<h3>Improving Accessibility in a Mature Estate</h3>
<p>As our population ages, more residents will rely on personal mobility
aids and wheelchairs. We are launching last-mile accessibility audits across
Queenstown, rallying residents and partners to help us assess how inclusive
and walkable our neighbourhood really is.</p>
<p>Even though Queenstown is an older estate, we’re committed to ensuring
it remains accessible and inclusive for all.</p>
<hr>
<h3>Creating Opportunities for Seniors to Keep Working</h3>
<p>Many seniors in Queenstown, especially those in blue-collar jobs, are
still active, sharp, and eager to contribute even after retirement. Work
offers more than income - it brings purpose, routine, and connection.</p>
<p>That’s why we’re setting up a Senior Employment Taskforce in Queenstown.
The taskforce will work with both seniors and employers to redesign jobs,
support training, and match seniors with meaningful work opportunities.</p>

<p>We need to see aging not as a period of decline, but as a new chapter
full of potential.</p>
<p>Here in Queenstown, we’re building a future where seniors are empowered
to live well, stay connected, and continue contributing to the community.
We believe Singapore can be a society that doesn’t just add years to life
- but life to years.</p>
