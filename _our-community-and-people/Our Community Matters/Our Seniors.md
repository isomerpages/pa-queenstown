<p>Dedicated to improving the quality of life for seniors in Queenstown by
developing initiatives that support healthy ageing, social inclusion, and
active participation in community life.</p>
<p><strong>Queenstown Seniors Festival 2024: A Celebration of Active Ageing</strong>
</p>
<p>As part of our ongoing commitment to support our seniors, the Senior Taskforce
proudly supported the Queenstown Seniors Festival 2024, a landmark event
organised by the Queenstown Citizens' Consultative Committee (CCC).</p>
<p>Event Highlights:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Date: 3rd and 4th August 2024</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Venue: Star Vista Atrium</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Attendance: Approximately 2,000 seniors and family members</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The festival showcased our community's dedication to enhancing the lives
of our elderly residents through three key exhibition segments:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Senior Home Living</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Senior in the Community</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Future Living of Seniors</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>These segments featured innovative solutions and services from esteemed
partners, including PA, HDB, AIC, Alexandra Hospital, and many others,
all aimed at improving seniors' quality of life.</p>
<p>Grassroots Adviser Mr Eric Chua graced the event, emphasising the importance
of community and government support in creating a senior-friendly environment.
He remarked, "The Seniors Festival showcases our dedication to our beloved
seniors and their families, and we hope it has inspired confidence for
healthy and purposeful longevity in our treasured red dot!"</p>
<p>The event provided a valuable platform for seniors and their families
to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Explore future senior living options</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Engage with informative exhibits</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Participate in enriching activities</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Gain insights into available resources and services</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The success of the Queenstown Seniors Festival 2024 reinforces our commitment
to supporting Queenstown's senior citizens. It sets a strong foundation
for future initiatives aimed at enhancing the lives of our elderly residents.</p>
<p>Get Involved The Senior Taskforce continues to work tirelessly to create
an inclusive and supportive environment for our seniors. If you'd like
to contribute or learn more about our initiatives, please <a href="https://queenstown.pa.gov.sg/contact-us/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">contact</a> us.</p>
<p>Together, we can build a community where our seniors thrive, staying active,
engaged, and valued members of Queenstown.</p>
