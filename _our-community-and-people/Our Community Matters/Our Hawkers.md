<h2>Supporting Our Hawkers in Queenstown</h2>
<p>Hawker centres have long been the soul of our neighbourhoods. In Queenstown,
we’re proud to have four of them: ABC Brickworks Market &amp; Food Centre,
Alexandra Village, Margaret Drive Hawker Centre, and Mei Ling Market &amp;
Food Centre. These are not just food places. They’re gathering points where
stories are shared, friendships are built, and generations come together
over a good, affordable meal. Keeping our hawker culture vibrant is therefore
essential.</p>
<h3>Queenstown HawkerFest 2023</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/HAWKERFEST/HAWKERFEST 2023/hawkerfest_banner.png">

<p>To celebrate this heritage and encourage greater footfall to our hawker
centres, we launched the first-ever Queenstown HawkerFest from 18 November
to 17 December 2023. The month-long celebration kicked off with a record-breaking
event - Singapore’s Largest Plate of Chicken Rice and continued with the
high-energy Chicken Chowdown featuring competitive eater Zermatt Neo and
Queenstown’s first-ever eating challenge.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<h4>Celebrating Flavours and Stories</h4>
<p>HawkerFest featured curated experiences like the Greatest Hits and Hawker
Café Remix, which encouraged visitors to explore familiar dishes and fresh
spins alike. Another highlight was the Hawker Heritage Trail - a chance
to walk down memory lane through Queenstown’s enduring legacy, and discover
the stories behind our estate’s iconic hawker centres. This trail connected
food to place, people to memory, and reminded us all of the value of preserving
and passing on this culture.</p>
<h3>Support for Hawkers</h3>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/Hawker/Screenshot_2025_05_29_at_10_21_51_AM.png">
</div>
<p>Link: <a href="https://mothership.sg/2024/02/bukit-merah-tuberculosis-cash/" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://mothership.sg/2024/02/bukit-merah-tuberculosis-cash/</a>
</p>
<p>Beyond celebration, we also stepped in to offer timely support. When tuberculosis
cases emerged around Jalan Bukit Merah, hawkers at ABC Market experienced
a sharp drop in footfall. Rental rebates were provided to help affected
stallholders tide through the slowdown and continue serving the community
without interruption. It was one of many efforts to ensure that our hawker
culture is not only appreciated, but protected.</p>
<p>Supporting our hawkers is something we do every day, by choosing to eat
local, by bringing others along, and by recognising the value of the stories
behind each dish. In Queenstown, we’re proud of our food heritage and even
prouder of the people who keep it alive. Let’s continue to show up for
them, not just during festivals, but in the quiet, everyday moments because
keeping our hawker culture alive starts with us.</p>
