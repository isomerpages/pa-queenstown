<h4><strong>Queenstown, Singapore – The Nation’s First Satellite Town</strong></h4>
<p>Queenstown holds the honour of being Singapore’s <strong>first satellite town</strong>,
developed in the 1950s by the Singapore Improvement Trust and later completed
by the Housing and Development Board (HDB). Named in commemoration of Queen
Elizabeth II's coronation, Queenstown was a pioneer in modern public housing,
featuring some of the country's earliest flats, schools, sports facilities,
and shopping centres.</p>
<p>With a blend of historical charm and continuous renewal, Queenstown remains
a meaningful symbol of Singapore’s urban development journey—where heritage
meets progress, and community spirit thrives across generations.</p>
