<h2><strong>A Message from Grassroots Adviser Eric Chua&nbsp;</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Dear Queenstown Residents,&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p>I am thrilled to connect with you and delighted to share more about the
significant impact the Queenstown Constituency Office has been making in
our community.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Serving the needs of our residents has been always been our topmost priority,
and we are proud to effect positive transformation in our constituency.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Enhancing Municipal Services&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Improving the living environment of our residents has always been a key
focus for us. We have worked tirelessly to enhance municipal services,
including upgrading of facilities, beautification projects, and infrastructure
improvements to create a more liveable and sustainable Queenstown.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Supporting Our Seniors&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Our seniors are the pillars of our community, and we strive to always
provide them with the care and support they deserve. Through various initiatives
such as Activity Aging Centres, health and wellness programmes and social
engagement activities, we want to create a vibrant and inclusive environment
for our seniors.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Empowering Our Youth&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>The youth are the future of Queenstown, and we have been actively engaging
and empowering our young residents through educational and skill-building
programmes, mentorship opportunities, and youth-centric events. We believe
in nurturing the potential of our young residents, providing them with
the resources they need to develop and thrive.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Preserving Our Heritage&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Queenstown boasts a rich and storied heritage and we have been resolute
in preserving and promoting our cultural legacy. Through heritage conservation
efforts, cultural events, and community outreach, we celebrate our history
and traditions, while embracing progress and modernity.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Fostering Multi-Racial Harmony&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Our constituency is a tapestry of diverse cultures and ethnicities, and
we take immense pride in fostering harmony and understanding among all
residents. We have organised various interfaith activities and and initiatives
to promote inclusivity and unity, ensuring that Queenstown remains a shining
example of multicultural co-existence.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Supporting Our Hawkers&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Our hawker centres are the heart of our local culinary scene, therefore
it is critical to provide support to our hawkers. Through exciting events
such as the HawkerFest 2023, various assistance programs, and initiatives,
we seek to preserve and promote the rich hawker culture that is integral
to the Queenstown experience.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Caring for Welfare&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>The welfare of our residents is of utmost importance to us. We have implemented
welfare assistance programs, financial aid schemes, and support services
to ensure that every resident in need receives the care and assistance
they require.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Building Resilience&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>In the face of challenges, we have strived to build resilience and social
capital within our community. Through emergency preparedness programs,
community bonding activities, and mental well-being initiatives, we aim
to ensure the safety and well-being of all residents.&nbsp;</p>
<h4><strong>Effective Communications&nbsp;</strong></h4>
<p>Effective communication is essential for a thriving community, and we
have been proactive in engaging with residents through various activities,
communication channels, and feedback platforms to ensure that your voices
are heard and addressed.&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
<p>As your Grassroots Adviser, I am committed to working hand in hand with
all of you to continue building a vibrant, inclusive, and resilient Queenstown.
Together, we can create a community that we are all proud to call home.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Thank you for your unwavering support and let us look forward to a brighter
future for Queenstown.&nbsp;</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>Warmest regards,&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Eric Chua&nbsp;</strong>
