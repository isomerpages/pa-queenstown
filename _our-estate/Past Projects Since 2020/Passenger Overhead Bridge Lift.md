<h3>Making Overhead Bridges Inclusive: Why Lifts Matter</h3>
<p>Overhead bridges help keep pedestrians safe by separating foot traffic
from busy roads. But for many seniors, wheelchair users, and people with
limited mobility, these bridges, especially those with only stairs can
be a major obstacle. That’s why lifts on overhead bridges are so important.</p>
<h4>Benefits of Overhead Bridge Lifts</h4>
<p><strong>1. Accessibility for All</strong>
<br>Lifts make it possible for wheelchair users, seniors with mobility aids,
and others with physical challenges to use overhead bridges independently.
No one should be left behind just because they can’t manage stairs.</p>
<p><strong>2. Safer and More Comfortable for Seniors</strong>
<br>As we grow older, stairs become harder to climb. Lifts reduce the risk
of falls and fatigue, giving seniors a safer, more comfortable way to cross
roads.</p>
<p><strong>3. Supporting Caregivers and Families</strong>
<br>Parents with strollers, caregivers with walking aids, or anyone carrying
groceries or luggage benefit from the convenience that lifts offer</p>
<p><strong>4. Encouraging Independence and Mobility</strong>
<br>With accessible infrastructure, more seniors and persons with disabilities
feel confident to get around, stay active, and take part in daily community
life.</p>
<p><strong>5. Building an Age- and Disability-Friendly City</strong>
<br>Adding lifts is part of our broader commitment to inclusivity. It ensures
that our urban environment meets the needs of all residents - not just
the able-bodied.</p>
