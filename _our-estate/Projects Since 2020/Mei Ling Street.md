<h3>Upgraded Fitness Corners for a Healthier Community</h3>
<p>Our upgraded fitness corners are designed to help residents stay active,
build connections, and enjoy the outdoors. With senior-friendly equipment,
shaded seating, and landscaped surroundings, these spaces encourage all
ages to:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Exercise regularly to stay healthy and mobile</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Meet neighbours and foster community bonds</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Relax and recharge in a pleasant, green setting</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>These enhancements support both physical and social well-being, right
at your doorstep.</p>
